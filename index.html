The LCAT pattern, four timed sections

Verbal 1 and 2

25 questions in 40 minutes each: words in context, text structure, evidence and data, inferences, grammar and punctuation, transitions, notes, and a long reading passage.

Math 1 and 2

20 questions in 40 minutes each, with no calculator: algebra, advanced math, data analysis, geometry and trigonometry, logarithms and sequences.

20 full mocks

90 questions and 160 minutes per mock. Each section has its own clock, and once a section closes you move on, as on the day.

What you get

  • Real timing. A 40-minute clock for each section, flags and an answer-sheet style question map.
  • Scores that help. Your result by section, Verbal and Math totals, and the topics to work on next.
  • Every answer explained. See the working or the rule, and why the tempting option is wrong.
  • A question bank by topic. Practice sorted by section, content domain and topic, separate from the 20 mocks.
  • Guide and rules. Who needs the LCAT, what to bring on test day, marking, formulas, grammar rules and study plans.
  • Private and offline. No account. Your scores stay on your phone.
